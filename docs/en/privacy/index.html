Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you play the Relic Layers mobile game (iOS and Android) and when you visit this website, why, on what legal basis, for how long, and what rights you have. It is also available in Polish.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Maciej Niklas, ul. Długa 23, 83-330 Skrzeszewo, Poland (“we”, “us”).

For all privacy matters, contact us at privacy@maciejniklas.com.

2. Summary

  • You can play without giving us your name or e-mail address. The game signs you in with a random player identifier (Player ID).
  • Your game progress is stored on Unity's servers so it can be verified and restored.
  • Gameplay analytics are sent only if you choose Allow analytics in the consent dialog. If you choose Play without analytics, no analytics events are sent.
  • If the game crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent to Unity so we can fix it.
  • This version of the game has no advertising and no in-app purchases, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Data processed by the game

Player account

When you first play online, the game creates an anonymous account with Unity Authentication. This gives you a random Player ID; sign-in tokens are stored on your device. Purpose: to identify your save data and protect it from other players. Legal basis: performance of the contract with you — providing the game you chose to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Game progress

Unity Cloud Save stores, under your Player ID: when your profile was created and last seen, your game language, whether your account is linked, the highest unlocked level, completed levels, recovered relics, and a record of level attempts (attempt identifiers, level and content version, start and completion times, and a fingerprint of the moves used to complete a level). Only the game's server code can change this data; it checks that a level was really completed before saving progress. Purpose: to save, verify and restore your progress and to keep progress fair. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); checking completions also serves our legitimate interest in preventing cheating and misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Optional account linking

You can link your progress to a Unity Player Account so it can be recovered on another device or after reinstalling. You sign in through Unity's own screen; the game uses only the sign-in tokens Unity returns and does not receive your password or store your e-mail address. The Unity Player Account itself is provided by Unity Technologies as a separate, independent controller under its own terms and privacy policy. If you do not link, your progress may not be recoverable after reinstalling the game or losing your device. Legal basis for linking in the game: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Configuration and game content

The game downloads settings (Unity Remote Config) and game content (Unity Cloud Content Delivery). These requests necessarily include technical data such as your IP address, app version, platform and technical identifiers. Purpose: to deliver the correct content and settings for your version of the game. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Providing this data is not a legal obligation, but the online features of the game cannot work without it. The first launch requires an internet connection to create your account.

4. Analytics and your consent

On first launch the game asks whether you allow optional gameplay analytics. Analytics are switched on only after you choose Allow analytics; we may also keep them switched off for everyone. If you choose Play without analytics, your choice is saved and no analytics events are sent. Opening this policy from the dialog does not count as a choice.

With your consent, Unity Analytics receives gameplay events: level started, completed or abandoned (with duration and number of actions), blocked moves, removed objects, tool use, recovered relics, steps of the account-linking prompt and content loading failures (with technical error codes). Unity Analytics also records a random installation identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and device and app information (for example device model, operating system and app version). Purpose: to understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to find problems. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

You can withdraw consent at any time. An in-game setting to change your choice is planned. Until it is available, you can clear the game's data or reinstall the game — the consent question is then shown again — or write to us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. You can also ask us to delete analytics data already collected (see section 11).

5. Crash and error reports

If the game crashes, freezes or encounters an error, Unity Diagnostics receives a technical report: the error and where in the code it happened, device and system information (for example device model, operating system version), app version, and the current level and content version. Reports do not include your Player ID or anything you type. Purpose: to find and fix errors that affect players. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable and working (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this processing (see section 11).

6. Data stored on your device

The game stores on your device: your settings (sound, haptics, language), your analytics choice, sign-in tokens, a count of attempts per level, completed levels waiting to be sent while you are offline, and downloaded game content. This storage is needed for the game to work, except the attempt count, which leaves your device only inside analytics events you agreed to. This data is removed when you clear the game's data or uninstall the game.

7. This website and contacting us

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising and loads no third-party resources. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (see the GitHub General Privacy Statement). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you write to us, we process your e-mail address, the content of your message and any data you provide, to answer you. Legal basis: handling requests about your rights is our legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR); other messages are handled in our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • This version of the game shows no ads, uses no advertising identifiers for advertising and has no in-app purchases. If we add advertising or purchases, we will update this policy before the update that introduces them.
  • The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Service providers and transfers

We use Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Code, Cloud Save, Remote Config, Cloud Content Delivery, Analytics and Diagnostics) to run the game's online features, and GitHub to host this website. They process data on our behalf, under data processing terms. Unity Player Accounts (section 3) is an independent service of Unity. More information: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Unity and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. According to Unity, analytics data is processed on servers in Belgium and stored in the Netherlands, with a replicated copy in the United States. For transfers outside the EEA, Unity's data processing addendum for Unity Gaming Services incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; GitHub describes its transfer safeguards in its privacy statement. You can ask us for information about these safeguards.

10. How long data is kept

  • Player account and game progress: until the account is deleted at your request, or until we permanently shut down the game's online services, after which we delete it.
  • Analytics: Unity deletes raw events after 13 months; aggregated metrics and the analytics profile are kept until deleted at your request or by us.
  • Crash and error reports: only as long as needed to investigate and fix the errors, and no longer than Unity Diagnostics keeps them.
  • Data on your device: until you clear the game's data or uninstall the game.
  • Messages and requests you send us: up to 3 years after the matter is closed, to show how we handled it and to defend against claims.

11. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to access your data and receive a copy, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl) — or with the authority in your country of residence or work.

To make a request, including deleting your account and progress, write to privacy@maciejniklas.com. Because the game does not collect your name or e-mail address, we will ask you for information that lets us find your game data, such as your Player ID. If we cannot link a request to game data, we will tell you. We respond within one month; this can be extended by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you why.

Deleting your account removes your Player ID and progress and cannot be undone; we also request deletion of analytics data that can be linked to you. An option to delete your account directly in the game is planned.

Your Unity Player Account (if you created one) is managed by Unity: you can delete it at player-account.unity.com.

12. Children

Relic Layers is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under 16 (or under the age at which you can consent to online services in your country), please ask a parent or guardian before choosing Allow analytics. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

13. Security

Data is sent between the game and Unity over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Progress can only be changed by the game's server code, not directly by the app. No system is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect to what the game needs and restrict access to it.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data processing changes, for example when new features are added, and change the “Last updated” date above. This address stays the same.
