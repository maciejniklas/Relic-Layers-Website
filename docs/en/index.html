Relic Layers

A portrait 3D puzzle game for iOS and Android about uncovering relics layer by layer.

Each level is a small diorama. Work out which pieces can come free, remove them in the right order and recover the relic hidden underneath.

Relic Layers is in development and not yet available in app stores.
