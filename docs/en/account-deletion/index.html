Delete your Relic Layers account

Last updated: 31 October 2026

You can request deletion of your Relic Layers player account and associated data by e-mail, even if you no longer have the game installed. Deletion cannot be undone.

Request deletion: privacy@maciejniklas.com

  1. Request deletion by e-mail
  2. Delete in the game
  3. What is deleted
  4. What remains and for how long
  5. Your separate Unity Player Account

1. Request deletion by e-mail

Write to privacy@maciejniklas.com and say that you want to delete your Relic Layers account and associated data. Include your Player ID, if available. In the game, open Settings → Account and use the Copy action next to Player ID before deleting your account.

If you cannot access the game or do not know your Player ID, you can still e-mail us. Explain that you cannot access it; we will discuss what information can help identify your account. We must be able to link the request to your data before deleting it. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens.

We respond within one month. For complex requests this can be extended by up to two further months; we will explain why. Sending an e-mail opens your mail application; this website does not submit a request for you.

2. Delete in the game

In the game, open Settings → Account → Delete account, read the consequences and confirm deletion. You need an internet connection. Cancelling the confirmation does not delete anything. If deletion fails, use the deletion option again or contact us by e-mail.

Relic Layers is currently in development and is not yet available in app stores. These steps describe the implemented in-game flow. Uninstalling the game alone does not delete your server account or progress.

3. What is deleted

  • Your Relic Layers player account in Unity Authentication, including its Player ID.
  • Your associated Unity Cloud Save data: profile, saved progress, recovered relics and level-attempt records.
  • We also request deletion of analytics data that can be linked to you, if any was collected. Unity states that analytics deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

After successful in-game deletion, the game clears the deleted player's local credentials, progress cache and pending synchronization data. Starting again creates a new anonymous account; it does not restore deleted progress.

4. What remains and for how long

  • E-mail correspondence and rights-request records: up to 3 years after the matter is closed, to demonstrate how it was handled and defend against claims.
  • Crash and error reports, which the game does not tag with your Player ID: only as long as needed to investigate and fix errors, and no longer than Unity Diagnostics retains them. Unity does not publish a fixed retention period in the documentation we use; we cannot promise a specific deletion date for these reports.
  • Shared downloaded content, device settings and consent preferences remain on your device after in-game account deletion. Clear the game's data or uninstall it to remove them.

For the full data inventory and retention rules, read our Privacy Policy. Account deletion is irreversible.

5. Your separate Unity Player Account

Deleting your Relic Layers account does not delete your separate Unity Player Account. If you created one, Unity manages it: use Unity's account-deletion page to delete that account separately.
